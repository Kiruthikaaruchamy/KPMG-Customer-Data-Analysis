# KPMG Customer Data Analysis using Excel

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing customer, transaction, and new customer data using Microsoft Excel. The analysis covers data cleaning, customer segmentation, transaction analysis, new customer insights, Customer Lifetime Value (CLV) analysis, and business recommendations.

## Objectives

The project was performed to transform raw customer and transaction data into meaningful business insights using Excel-based data analysis and visualization techniques.

## Tools Used

- Microsoft Excel
- Pivot Tables
- Excel Formulas
- Charts and Visualizations
- Data Cleaning and Transformation

## Analysis Performed

1. Removed duplicate records from customer address data and standardized state names.

2. Cleaned customer demographic data by correcting erroneous entries, standardizing missing-data formats, and resolving inconsistencies in gender representation.

3. Cleaned transaction data by standardizing the transaction date format and removing records with missing or incomplete information.

4. Standardized new customer data by formatting addresses, gender representation, job titles, and job industry categories.

5. Performed customer segmentation by analyzing customer counts and average tenure across wealth segments.

6. Analyzed customer segments by gender using customer counts and average past three years of bike-related purchases.

7. Analyzed customer distribution across job industry categories and examined wealth segment distribution within each industry.

8. Analyzed monthly sales trends and identified seasonal patterns and significant sales spikes.

9. Evaluated product performance by analyzing total sales by brand and total sales and average list price by product line.

10. Analyzed customer purchase behavior by identifying the top 10 customers based on total transaction value and calculating the average number of purchases per customer.

11. Analyzed new customer demographics based on wealth segment, job industry category, and average past three years of bike-related purchases.

12. Performed new customer location analysis by examining customer distribution by state and the relationship between property valuation and wealth segment.

13. Estimated potential revenue from new customers using past three years of bike-related purchases and customer value.

14. Calculated Customer Lifetime Value (CLV) using Average Purchase Value, Purchase Frequency, and Customer Lifespan.

15. Performed CLV segmentation by comparing average CLV across wealth segments and analyzing its relationship with customer demographics.

16. Consolidated key findings from customer segmentation, transaction analysis, new customer insights, and CLV analysis.

17. Developed marketing recommendations based on high-value customer segments.

18. Identified potential business expansion opportunities based on new customer location analysis.

19. Proposed product improvement opportunities based on transaction and product performance analysis.

## Customer Lifetime Value

Customer Lifetime Value was calculated using:

**CLV = (Average Purchase Value × Purchase Frequency) × Customer Lifespan**

Where:

- Average Purchase Value = Total Revenue / Number of Purchases
- Purchase Frequency = Total Number of Transactions / Number of Unique Customers
- Customer Lifespan = Customer Tenure
